About

I'm in the last semester of my MSc in Data Science at the University of Mannheim; my thesis was submitted on 15 September 2026 and I'm waiting for the grade. Since February 2024 I've also worked as a working student at Neox Networks, where I built a RAG chatbot and search system that the sales team uses for customer questions.

My university projects are mostly about text: a retrieval pipeline that fills in missing product data, LLM annotation of German news checked against human coders, a coping-strategy classifier trained with active learning from 100 to 200 labels, and a multi-agent shopping assistant. My Master's thesis, with the industry partner Istari.ai, looked at semantic company search and at how to evaluate it when the only labels come from the production system.

Something I've learned along the way is that the first evaluation setup is often wrong: an abstention rule tuned for one model, an accuracy number inflated by class imbalance, a benchmark that scores against the system you're trying to beat. The case studies below include those problems and the results that didn't work out.
