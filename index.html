About

I'm finishing my MSc in Data Science at the University of Mannheim. Most of what I build sits at the intersection of transformer fine-tuning, retrieval, and LLM agents - systems that have to make a defensible call on messy, real-world, mostly-unlabeled text, and then prove it against a real baseline.

That's shown up as fine-tuning EmoBERTa with active learning from 100-200 labeled examples, a multi-agent LLM system that has to justify its recommendations, a retrieval-augmented pipeline that recovers missing product data instead of letting an LLM hallucinate it, and an embedding-model benchmark across 20 million companies for an industry thesis partner.

I track new models and techniques closely, but the real test is always whether a result holds up under a baseline nobody asked me to run - which is why every case study below includes one, plus the negative results I didn't have to report.
