About

I'm finishing my MSc in Data Science at the University of Mannheim. Across three years of coursework and an industry thesis, one question keeps coming back: how do you get a model to make a genuinely reliable call on messy, real-world, mostly-unlabeled data - and then prove it, not just claim it?

That question has taken different shapes - active learning on 200 labeled grief-journal entries, a multi-agent LLM shopping copilot that has to justify its picks, a RAG pipeline that imputes missing product specs instead of hallucinating them, and a semantic search benchmark over 20 million companies for an industry thesis partner.

I care less about which model is trendiest and more about whether the result holds up under a baseline nobody asked me to run - which is why most of these case studies include one, plus the negative results I didn't have to report.
