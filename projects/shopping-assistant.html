LLMs & Agents

A multi-agent shopping assistant built with LangGraph

You type something like "quiet stand mixer under $150" and get three ranked products with a short explanation for each. Against human-annotated rankings for 20 queries, it reached Precision@10 of 0.565, compared with 0.315 for keyword search.

Problem Approach Results Model choice

The problem

Comparing products online is tedious: spec sheets are inconsistent, a 4.8 rating from four reviews is not the same as 4.6 from four hundred, and many listings are near duplicates. We wanted a tool that takes a plain-language request and a budget and returns a few options with reasons instead of a sorted list.

The approach

The LangGraph pipeline has five steps: process_query → search_products → extract_specifications → rank_products → generate_recommendations.

  • Search: SerpAPI returns up to 20 Google Shopping results per query, and Tavily adds spec sheets and review text.
  • Ranking: the score takes the number of reviews into account, so a product with a few very positive reviews does not automatically beat one with hundreds.
  • Interface: a Streamlit app with product cards, score breakdowns and CSV export. A full query usually finishes within 30 seconds.

Model choice

We started with Gemini 2.0 Flash. The free tier allowed 15 requests per minute, while one query needs up to 20 product analyses, and it often returned malformed JSON even with strict formatting instructions, which broke the parsing steps. DeepSeek-Coder, tested as a local option, handled short structured prompts but wrote shallow product summaries. We ended up with Llama 3.1 running locally through Ollama.
